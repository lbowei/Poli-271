How it works

  1. Share your idea

  2. Get a quote

Compliance & transparency

Paperwork your buyers and customs will ask for

amfori BSCI audited

Our most recent social audit was carried out by TÜV Rheinland in December 2025. Buyers can request the full report and our corrective-action progress.

Cotton traceability

For US-bound orders, we use fabric from mills that supply cotton-origin documents from bale to yarn to fabric, with non-Xinjiang cotton, to support your UFLPA due diligence.

Environmental registration

Our site is registered in China's pollutant discharge system, valid through 2030. Wet processing is done at licensed laundries in a designated industrial park.

Labeling for your market

We prepare fiber-content, country-of-origin and care labels to the rules of your market (US FTC, EU, UK, Australia).

Importing into the United States?

We quote FOB by default. You are the importer of record, so US duties are charged on the FOB value, not on your retail price. Ask us for a landed-cost worksheet with current duty rates for your product's HTS code. Rates have changed often since 2025, so we check them for every quote.

FAQ

Questions brands ask us

Do you work with first-time brands?

Yes. Most of our direct clients are small brands. We'll help you sort out your tech pack, pick fabrics and washes, and plan a first order your budget can carry.

Can you develop a style from a reference garment?

Yes. Send us the garment or detailed photos and measurements. Our sample room will make the pattern, and we'll confirm fit with you before bulk production.

Can I mix sizes and colors within the MOQ?

Yes. The MOQ is per style, and it can be split across a full size run and up to two colors or washes.

How do US tariffs and customs work with you?

On FOB terms you import the goods and pay duties on the FOB value. Every quote comes with a landed-cost estimate, and we can arrange DDP shipping through partner forwarders if you'd rather receive the goods duty-paid.

Can you provide cotton origin documents?

Yes. For US-bound orders we put together a traceability file (fabric, yarn and cotton invoices plus certificates of origin) that you can keep on hand for customs.

Can we visit or audit the factory?

Yes. On-site visits, live video tours and third-party inspections are all welcome.

Will you protect my designs?

Yes. We'll sign an NDA, and your designs, patterns and labels are never offered to other clients.
