How it works

From inquiry to shipment in about eight weeks.

A typical first order, shown week by week. Reorders skip sampling, so they're faster.

  1. Share your idea

    Send a tech pack, a reference garment or a sketch, plus your target quantity and price.

  2. Get a quote

    FOB pricing by quantity tier, with a landed-cost estimate if you're importing into the US.

  3. Sample

    We send photos and measurements before the sample ships, so the fit call goes faster.

  4. Approve

    Fit comments, a pre-production sample and a signed-off wash standard.

  5. Produce

    Bulk production starts once the PP sample is approved and the deposit has arrived. You get progress photos each week.

  6. Ship

    Final QC, then FOB Shenzhen/Guangzhou, or DDP through our partner forwarders.

Importing into the US?

We quote FOB by default. You're the importer of record, so US duties are charged on the FOB value, not your retail price. Duty rates have changed often since 2025, so every quote comes with a landed-cost worksheet that uses the current rates for your HTS code.

FAQ

Questions brands ask us.

Do you work with first-time brands?

Yes, most of our direct clients are small brands. We'll help you clean up your tech pack, pick fabrics and washes, and plan a first order that fits your budget.

Can you develop a style from a reference garment?

Yes. Send us the garment, or detailed photos and measurements. Our sample room will make the pattern, and we'll confirm the fit with you before bulk.

Can I mix sizes and colors within the MOQ?

Yes. The MOQ is per style, and you can split it across a full size run and up to two colors or washes.

How do US tariffs and customs work with you?

On FOB terms you import the goods and pay duty on the FOB value. Every quote comes with a landed-cost estimate, and if you'd rather receive the goods duty-paid, we can arrange DDP shipping through a partner forwarder.

Can you provide cotton-origin documents?

Yes. For US-bound orders we prepare a traceability file with fabric, yarn and cotton invoices plus certificates of origin, which you keep on hand for customs.

Can we visit or audit the factory?

Yes. On-site visits, live video tours and third-party inspections are all welcome.

Will you protect my designs?

Yes. We'll sign an NDA, and your designs, patterns and labels are never offered to other clients.
